**Purpose.** De-risks the purchase. A buyer who understands exactly how the work will run is far easier to close than one who is asked to trust a brand.

**Contains.**
- The four phases: discovery, prototype, production, handover
- What you receive at the end of each phase, and the go or stop decision between them
- Cadence: weekly demo, written decision records, one named lead
- Tooling and access we need from you, and the access we do not need
- How scope changes are handled without a change request fight
- What we expect from your team, stated plainly

**Primary CTA.** Book a call to walk through the method against your project.
