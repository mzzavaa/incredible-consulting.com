**Purpose.** States the exit up front. Buyers trust a consultancy more when it explains how it leaves.

**Contains.**
- Documentation set: architecture, decisions, runbook, known limitations
- Live walkthrough with the receiving team, recorded
- Ownership map: who owns what after we go
- Optional support tail and what it covers
- Deeper enablement, if the team needs skills rather than handover notes
- The retainer option, if ongoing senior input is wanted

**Primary CTA.** Ask what handover looks like for your stack.
**Links to.** /engagements/retainer/, and https://incredible.training/for-teams/enablement-programmes/ for team enablement after handover.
