**Purpose.** Stops us building the wrong thing well. This is where most failed AI projects were actually lost.

**Contains.**
- Stakeholder interviews and the questions we always ask
- Data reality check: what exists, what is usable, what is a rumour
- Constraint mapping: regulatory, technical, organisational
- Use case ranking by value against feasibility
- The success metric, agreed in writing and in numbers
- Explicit output: a decision on whether to proceed

**Primary CTA.** Start with a discovery workshop.
