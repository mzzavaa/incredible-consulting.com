The first conversation is a free 30 minute idea call. You get clarity and a
concrete next step out of it whether or not anything is ever bought. It is held
with the person who would run the engagement, because a call with someone who
would then hand you over is a waste of both halves of the hour.

## What the 30 minutes are for

- **Your idea or problem, described in your own words.** No template, no form to
  fill in first.
- **A read on feasibility.** Which parts are straightforward, which part is the
  actually hard one, and what data it depends on.
- **A shape and a rough number.** Which [engagement model](/engagements/) fits,
  and roughly what it costs, using the published
  [prices](/pricing/).
- **A funding check.** Whether the work could qualify for cloud provider funding.
  See [Cloud funding and credits](/pricing/funding/).
- **A next step,** even if the next step is that you do it yourselves and here is
  how.

## What it is not

Not a pitch, not a qualification interview, and not a discovery workshop
delivered for free. A real workshop needs preparation, the right people in the
room, and several hours. This call is how you decide whether to book one.

## What to bring

The problem, in one or two sentences. What has already been tried. Any hard
constraint you already know about: a deadline, a regulator, a system that cannot
be touched, a budget ceiling. If you have none of that yet, come anyway; working
out what the constraint is can be the whole value of the call.

## Practical details

- 30 minutes, free, no obligation.
- German or English.
- Vienna time, Europe/Vienna. Slots outside those hours by arrangement.
- Video call by default, phone if you prefer.
- Nothing is recorded.

## What happens afterwards

A short written summary within two working days: what was discussed, what was
recommended, and if it is relevant, a proposal outline with a price and a start
date. If the honest recommendation is that you do not need us, that is what the
summary will say.

## If you would rather write first

Email works, and it is read by the same person. For teams that already have a
defined scope and want a written proposal, use
[Request a proposal](/contact/request-a-proposal/) instead: it collects enough
detail to skip straight past the exploratory stage.
