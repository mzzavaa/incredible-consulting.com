Two documents, both held in one place across the group so that a change is made
once rather than seven times.

- [**Impressum**](/legal/imprint/). The disclosure required under the Austrian
  E-Commerce-Gesetz, Mediengesetz, Gewerbeordnung and Unternehmensgesetzbuch.
  Deliberately in German: the obligations are Austrian, and the German text is the
  one that discharges them.
- [**Privacy**](/legal/privacy/). What this site does with your data, which is
  nothing, with an English summary at the end.

## The short version of the privacy notice

This is a static site. No cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no tag manager,
no tracking pixels, and no forms, so nothing is submitted to us. No external
stylesheets, fonts or scripts, so your browser makes no third-party requests while
reading it. Outbound links, including the booking link, are followed only when you
click them, and the receiving provider's terms then apply.

If any of that changes, the privacy notice is wrong and gets updated first.

## Contracts and procurement

Commercial terms, the contracting entity and the vendor pack are not legal notices
and live on [Procurement and contracting](/pricing/procurement/).

## The rest of the group

Each Incredible domain keeps its own imprint and privacy notice where the law
requires one, and the group index sits on incredible-company.com.
