Three groups of questions: how the work runs, what it costs and how it is
contracted, and what happens to your data. Each has its own page. The five most
common are answered here.

## Do you only do AI projects?

No. Many engagements start as an AI question and turn out to be a cloud, data or
process problem. We follow the highest-leverage path rather than the one that
matches the label on the enquiry, and we say when the AI part was decoration.

## What if we stop after the prototype?

That is a good outcome. Stopping a weak use case early saves the budget and
prevents months of rework. The kill criteria are agreed at the start precisely so
that stopping does not require anyone to lose an argument.

## Who actually does the work?

The person who runs the scoping call. The named lead is in the proposal before
anything is signed and does not change without your agreement. Where a specialist
is needed, they come from the [associate network](/team/network/), are named, and
you meet them before they start.

## Do you work in German and English?

Yes. Workshops for teams in Austria and Germany usually run in German, and
documentation is delivered in whichever language the receiving team reads.

## What does it cost?

Published, with the assumptions attached: about EUR 2,000 per workshop session,
about EUR 1,000 per result document, EUR 250 per hour, and packages from about
EUR 4,000 to about EUR 16,000. See [Pricing](/pricing/). A good share of this work
also qualifies for [cloud provider funding](/pricing/funding/).
