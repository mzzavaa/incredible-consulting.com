**Purpose.** Technical buyers want to know the stack before the call. This section answers that
without a discovery meeting, including the parts we bring in specialists for.

**Contains.**
- Primary depth and where it is genuinely deepest: AWS, including AWS Community Hero recognition
- Full hyperscaler coverage through the associate network: AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, with the
  honest statement of which we are strongest on rather than a claim of equal mastery everywhere
- Local and self-hosted AI: running models on your own infrastructure, when that is the right answer
  (sovereignty, data residency, cost at volume, latency) and when it is an expensive detour
- The platform engineering layer, linking to /platforms/developer-platforms/
- Model and vendor selection: how we choose, and how we avoid lock-in we cannot exit
- Where we are deliberately vendor-neutral, and how a selection assessment stays that way when we
  have a visible AWS relationship
- What we will not build on, and why
- Community credentials rather than partner-tier badges, stated as what they are
