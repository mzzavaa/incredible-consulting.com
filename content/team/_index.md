Buyers of small consultancies are afraid of two things: that the person in the
pitch will disappear after signing, and that the work will quietly be handed to
whoever is free. Both happen often enough that the fear is reasonable.

Incredible Consulting is built so that neither can happen. Every engagement has
one named lead who runs the scoping call, writes the proposal, and does or
directs the work. That lead is named in the proposal before anything is signed,
and does not change without your agreement.

## How an engagement is staffed

Most projects need one or two senior people, not a team of six. A workshop series
is one facilitator. A proof of concept is usually one builder with a second pair
of eyes on architecture and security. A production hardening phase may add a
platform specialist for a few weeks.

When a project genuinely needs depth that is not in house, a specialist comes in
from the [associate network](/team/network/), is introduced to you by name before
they start, and is contracted through us so there is one invoice and one point of
liability.

When there is no capacity and no suitable associate, we say so and decline. That
is uncomfortable and it is still cheaper for everyone than padding an engagement
to fill a gap.

## Masters, and what the word means here

The group uses the word Master rather than consultant on purpose. A Master is a
practitioner admitted to the roster on the strength of what they have shipped in
production, not on a certification count or a sales target. Membership is by
introduction, and it is reviewed rather than permanent.

The roster is small today and is intended to grow. Independent specialists who
want to be considered will find the admission standard, the disciplines currently
open, and how contracting works on the
[associate network](/team/network/) page.

## Languages, location and reach

German and English. Based in Austria, delivering across the DACH region and the
EU, on site where it helps and remote where it does not. Workshops in Vienna and
the surrounding region are usually on site because a room full of the right
people is worth the travel. Build phases are usually remote because they do not
benefit from it.
