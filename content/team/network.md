Every mid-size buyer asks a boutique the same question: can you actually staff
this. The honest answer is that one person cannot, and that a body shop staffed
with whoever is on the bench is not an improvement. The associate network is the
middle path: a small group of independent specialists, each admitted for one
discipline they have demonstrably shipped in production, brought in by name and
for a defined piece of work.

## What the network covers

- **AWS.** Primary depth, held in house. Bedrock and agent runtimes, serverless
  architecture, data services, cost engineering.
- **Azure and Google Cloud.** Covered through associates rather than claimed as
  equal in house depth. Where a platform selection is genuinely open, that is
  stated before the assessment starts.
- **Local and self-hosted AI.** Open weight models on your own hardware, for
  sovereignty, residency, latency, or cost at volume.
- **Platform engineering.** Backstage, Red Hat Developer Hub, Ansible and Ansible
  Automation Platform, OpenShift on cloud or on premises.
- **Data engineering.** Ingestion, modelling, quality and lineage.
- **Security.** Review, threat modelling, and penetration testing through named
  specialist partners.

## How associates are selected

Three conditions, all of them necessary:

1. **Shipped in production.** Not a lab, not a certification, not a conference
   talk about a thing that was never operated. The reference is a system that ran,
   with users, and that the person can describe including what went wrong.
2. **One discipline, stated plainly.** An associate is admitted for the thing they
   are genuinely excellent at. Generalists are useful and are not what this
   network is for.
3. **Direct working relationship.** We have worked with them, or with someone who
   has and will say so on the record.

Membership is reviewed, not permanent, and either side can end it.

## How you meet them

An associate is named in the proposal, with the specific scope they cover, before
you sign. You meet them before they start. If you would rather not work with a
particular person, say so and we find another route or scope the work down.

## Contracting, liability and confidentiality

You contract with us, not with the associate. One statement of work, one invoice,
one party liable. Associates are engaged under a back-to-back agreement that
carries your confidentiality terms, your data processing terms, and your
intellectual property terms through unchanged. Subprocessors and the group trust
position are documented on the group site, linked from
[Procurement and contracting](/pricing/procurement/).

## Where we say no

We do not subcontract blindly to fill a gap, we do not resell offshore delivery
capacity, and we do not staff a role we cannot review the output of. If a project
needs a discipline nobody in the network holds, the correct answer is a referral
to a firm that does, and we give it.

## Joining the roster

The roster is small and is meant to grow. Independent consultants, freelancers,
and small studios working in the disciplines above can ask to be considered.

What to send: what you have shipped in production, in which role, on which
platform, and one thing about it that did not work and what you did about it.
What not to send: a certification list.

What happens next: a conversation, a technical reference check, and if it fits, a
first engagement scoped small enough that both sides can walk away from it
cleanly.

Introductions go to the same address as everything else on this site, marked for
the network. Employment inside the group, as opposed to independent association,
is handled on the group site rather than here.
