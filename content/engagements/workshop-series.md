**Purpose.** For leadership teams that need alignment and a decision, not a training course.

**Contains.**
- Session structure: framing, use case mining, feasibility, prioritisation, roadmap
- Who should be in the room, and who must not be
- Outputs: ranked use case list, effort and value estimates, target architecture sketch, risks
- Format: on site in Vienna and the DACH region, or remote
- What happens after: PoC scope, or a written recommendation to stop
- Sample agenda download slot

**Primary CTA.** Request a workshop proposal.
