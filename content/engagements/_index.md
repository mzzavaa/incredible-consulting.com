Buyers usually know their problem and not the shape of the agreement. This page
turns "we should probably do something with AI" into a scope, a duration and a
number.

## Which one fits

- **No clarity yet.** You have an ambition, several ideas, and no agreement on
  which one is real. Start with a [workshop series](/engagements/workshop-series/).
- **Clarity, no proof.** You know the use case and need evidence before a budget
  cycle. Start with a [proof of concept](/engagements/proof-of-concept/).
- **An inherited or ageing system.** You need an independent read you can show a
  board. Start with an [architecture audit](/engagements/architecture-audit/).
- **Proof, no senior owner.** The direction is agreed and there is nobody to hold
  it. Start with [fractional leadership](/engagements/fractional-leadership/).
- **A system that is live.** It works and now needs a senior voice on call.
  Start with a [retainer](/engagements/retainer/).

## The five models

| Model | Typical duration | Price basis |
|---|---|---|
| Workshop series | 1 day, or 2 to 3 sessions with gaps | About EUR 2,000 per session, packages from about EUR 4,000 |
| Proof of concept | 4 to 10 weeks | Fixed scope, fixed fee |
| Architecture audit | 2 to 4 weeks | Fixed fee |
| Fractional leadership | 3 to 12 months, rolling | Monthly, by days per week |
| Retainer | 6 or 12 month terms | Monthly block of days |

Full figures and the assumptions behind them are on [Pricing](/pricing/).

## Combining them

The common path is a workshop series into a proof of concept, with the workshop
fee credited against the build. The second most common is an audit that turns into
a fixed-scope remediation. Nothing here requires starting at the beginning: if you
already know what you want built, say so and skip to a proposal.

## What we do not do

- Staff augmentation. We do not sell days into your backlog.
- Unmanaged offshore delivery.
- Long-term operations of a system your team should own. Handover is a phase, not
  a farewell.
- Vendor-locked implementations we cannot hand back.
- Strategy decks without validation.

A free 30 minute call, with the person who would do the work. It ends with a
