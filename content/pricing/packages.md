A package is a fixed number of [sessions](/pricing/) and [results](/pricing/)
bought together, with the scope agreed before the first one runs. It exists so
that a buyer who already knows roughly what they need does not have to sit
through a scoping exercise to get a number.

## Discovery Package

**One session, two results. About EUR 4,000.**

For a team with an ambition and no agreed scope. One workshop to define the goal
and find the use cases, and two written results, typically a goal and use case
definition plus a first technical concept.

Ends with: a ranked shortlist of use cases and an honest read on whether any of
them is worth building.

## Concept Package

**One session, three results. About EUR 5,000.**

For a team that has already decided roughly what it wants and needs the concept
written down well enough to be reviewed, costed and defended internally.

Ends with: a business concept, a technical concept, and a third artefact of your
choice, usually a user story map or a cost model.

## Prototype Package

**Two sessions, three results. About EUR 7,000.**

For a team that needs to see the thing work. Two workshops, one to define and one
to review, and three results including a working prototype or a recorded demo
built on your own data.

Ends with: something running that people can try, plus the concept behind it.

## Comprehensive Package

**Four sessions, eight results. About EUR 16,000.**

The full run from idea to a decision about production: discovery, concept,
prototype, and an ROI and roadmap session, with the supporting documents for
each.

Ends with: a prototype, a cost estimate for the production version, and a roadmap
with a go or stop recommendation attached.

## What every package includes

- Preparation and research before each session
- Written documentation of what was decided, not just a photograph of a whiteboard
- One feedback round per result
- A next-step recommendation, including the recommendation to stop
- A funding check: much of this work is fundable, see
  [Cloud funding and credits](/pricing/funding/)

## What no package includes

- Production hardening, operations, and go-live. That is an
  [engagement](/engagements/), not a package.
- MVP development with your internal team or an implementation partner. Scoped
  separately, usually hourly or as a fixed-scope build.
- Licence, infrastructure and model inference costs. Those are yours, and the
  estimate for them is one of the results.

## Assumptions behind the prices

Up to five participants per workshop, and one to three use cases per result
document. Beyond that the scope changes and so does the fee. Larger groups,
multiple stakeholder organisations, or a domain requiring significant preparatory
research are quoted individually.

## Converting a package into a larger engagement

If a package turns into a build, the package fee is credited against the first
invoice of the follow-on engagement. There is no penalty for stopping instead:
that is a legitimate outcome and it is one of the reasons the packages are priced
this way.
