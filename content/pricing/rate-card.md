All figures are net, exclude VAT, and are valid for 2026.

## The rates

- **Workshop session, two to four hours: about EUR 2,000.** Includes
  preparation, research, documentation and a next-step recommendation.
- **Result document or artefact: about EUR 1,000.** Includes one feedback round.
- **Hourly: EUR 250.** Ad hoc consulting, deep dives, reviews, one to one
  support sessions.
- **Packages: about EUR 4,000 to about EUR 16,000.** See
  [Packages](/pricing/packages/).

## Why there is no published day rate

The unit sold here is a session or a result, not a day of someone's attendance.
A day rate invites the question of how many days a thing takes, which is the
wrong question and the one that makes consulting expensive. Where a genuine
time-and-materials arrangement is the right structure, it is priced from the
hourly rate above and the estimate is written into the statement of work.

## Retainers and fractional leadership

Priced as a monthly block of days, derived from the hourly rate, with a reduction
that scales with the size of the block and the length of the commitment. The
figure is in the proposal, together with the rollover rule for unused days and
the notice period. See [Retainer](/engagements/retainer/) and
[Fractional leadership](/engagements/fractional-leadership/).

## Fixed-scope work

Architecture audits, proofs of concept and defined builds are quoted as a fixed
fee against a written scope, with the assumptions and the exclusions listed in
the same document. A fixed fee moves only when the scope moves, and then in
writing before the work continues.

## What changes a quote

- More than five participants in a workshop
- More than three use cases in a single result document
- Several stakeholder organisations in the same room
- A domain requiring significant preparatory research
- Access constraints that make the work slower, for example a locked-down
  environment where every change needs a ticket

Each of these is named in the proposal rather than discovered later.

## Expenses and travel

Travel within Vienna is not charged. Travel beyond it is charged at cost, agreed
in advance, and only when on-site presence is actually useful. Accommodation and
subsistence at cost, no per diem markup.

## Payment terms

Net 14 days from invoice, EUR, excluding VAT. Longer engagements are invoiced
monthly in arrears against delivered sessions and results. Packages are invoiced
half at the start and half on delivery of the last result.

Reverse charge applies for EU business customers with a valid VAT identification
number. Details, the legal entity, and the contracting documents are on
[Procurement and contracting](/pricing/procurement/).

## Cancellation

A scheduled workshop moved with more than five working days notice costs nothing.
Inside five working days, half the session fee is due, because the preparation
has already been done and the day cannot be resold. This applies in both
directions: if a session is moved from our side inside that window, the next one
is free.

## Reduced rates

Non-profit organisations, community programmes and public-interest work are
quoted at a reduced rate. Open methodology and material published for free live
on the sibling site rather than being sold here.
