This page exists for legal and procurement teams, who can hold a
signed-in-principle engagement for a month. Most of what they need can be sent in
advance.

## The entity

The contracting party is an Austrian sole proprietorship operating under a
registered trade for IT consulting and data processing services, supervised by the
Bezirkshauptmannschaft Güssing. Full statutory details are in the
[Impressum](/legal/imprint/).

Practical consequences worth knowing early: one contracting party, one invoice,
and one person liable. Where associates are involved they are engaged by us, not
by you. See [Associate network](/team/network/).

## The contract stack

- **NDA.** Signed before anything sensitive is shared. Yours as-is in almost all
  cases.
- **Master services agreement.** For ongoing relationships. Optional for a single
  fixed-scope engagement, where the statement of work is enough on its own.
- **Statement of work.** Scope, deliverables, price, assumptions, exclusions,
  timeline and the named lead. This is the document that matters, and the
  exclusions section is the part worth reading first.
- **Data processing agreement.** Signed where the work involves personal data,
  with the subprocessors named rather than referenced generically.

Your paper is generally acceptable. Where a clause cannot be signed as written,
you get the specific clause and the reason rather than a blanket refusal.

## Intellectual property

Work product is yours on payment. Code sits in your repository from the first
commit. Where a pre-existing pattern, library or component of ours is used, you
receive a licence to use and modify it without restriction, and that is written
into the statement of work rather than left to be discovered later.

Nothing from an engagement is published, presented or reused as public material
without written permission, which is why no client is named anywhere on this site.

## Supplier onboarding

Supplier questionnaires, portal registrations and vendor forms are completed
rather than argued with. Where a portal demands something that does not apply to
an entity this size, you get a clear written explanation rather than a blank
field.

Common requests that can be sent immediately: entity details, trade registration,
bank details on letterhead, references on request, and the security position
described on [Security and compliance](/faq/security-and-compliance/).

## Public tenders

Answered where the requirement is a genuine fit and the process is proportionate
to the size of the work. Where it is not, you get a straight no rather than a
compliant response nobody believes. For public bodies, scoping an engagement to
sit inside a direct award threshold is often the more useful conversation. See
[Public sector](/industries/public-sector/).
