Most consulting sites answer the price question with a contact form. This one
answers it with numbers. Publishing them costs a few enquiries and saves a great
deal of everybody's time.

There are three units of purchase, and they combine.

## Buy a session

A facilitated working session of two to four hours with the people who own the
problem. **About EUR 2,000 per session.**

The fee includes preparation, the research behind it, written documentation of
what was decided, and a next-step recommendation. It is not an hourly charge for
the time in the room.

## Buy a result

A defined artefact, delivered and then revised once after your feedback.
**About EUR 1,000 per result.**

A result is a business concept, a technical concept, a user story map, a simple
prototype or demo, a cost model, or a comparable document or artefact agreed in
advance. One feedback round is included.

## Buy hours

Ad hoc consulting, deep dives, reviews, one to one support sessions.
**EUR 250 per hour.**

Used when the work does not fit a session or a result: a second opinion on an
architecture, a code review, an interview panel, a vendor conversation.

## Or buy a package

Four pre-scoped combinations of sessions and results, from about EUR 4,000 to
about EUR 16,000. See [Packages](/pricing/packages/).

## What moves a price

The figures above assume up to five participants per workshop and one to three
use cases per result document. Larger groups, more use cases, several
stakeholder organisations in one room, or a domain that needs significant
preparatory research move the price up, and that is said in the proposal rather
than discovered on the invoice.

Longer engagements, retainers and fractional arrangements are priced on
[Rates and terms](/pricing/rate-card/). Fixed-scope work, including the
[architecture audit](/engagements/architecture-audit/), is quoted as a fixed fee
against a written scope.

## What is not charged for

- The first call. Thirty minutes, free, spent on your problem.
- The proposal.
- Travel within Vienna.
- The correction of our own mistakes.

## Funding

Cloud and AI work frequently qualifies for provider funding: cash contributions
toward consulting for proofs of concept, and larger programmes for migrations.
Applying for it is part of the engagement, not an extra. See
[Cloud funding and credits](/pricing/funding/).

## Terms

All figures are net and exclude VAT. Invoicing is in EUR. Payment terms, the
legal entity, contracting documents, and the vendor pack are on
[Procurement and contracting](/pricing/procurement/).
